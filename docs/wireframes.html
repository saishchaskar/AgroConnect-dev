Guide content needs local review

Tomato · general guide

Use this outline as a starting point. Follow current local agricultural guidance for your district and season.

Before planting

Choose a variety suited to your season and market. Check the local sowing calendar and use healthy seedlings from a trusted source.

Planting and care

Follow locally recommended spacing. Keep soil moisture steady, avoid waterlogging, and check plants regularly for changes.

Crop health checks

Look at several plants, including the underside of leaves. Take clear photos and note when symptoms first appeared.

When you need help

Ask a local agriculture officer or Krishi Vigyan Kendra to confirm a problem before acting on treatment advice.

Source, district suitability, and last-reviewed date must be shown before publishing crop-specific advice.
